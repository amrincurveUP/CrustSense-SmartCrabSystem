Species Guide

Electric Blue Crayfish

Procambarus alleni

  • Ideal temperature: 18–24°C
  • Needs clean, well-oxygenated water
  • Active at dawn and dusk — check movement daily
  • Molts periodically — provide hiding spots
  • Feed once daily; remove leftovers within 2 hours
  • Blue colour brightens with good water quality
Basket L1 & L3

Neon Crayfish

Procambarus clarkii

  • Ideal temperature: 24–30°C
  • Tolerates slightly turbid water; clean is still better
  • Check claws and antennae for damage or fungus
  • More aggressive — allow enough space per basket
  • Protein-rich diet; avoid overfeeding
  • Colour fades when stressed or water quality drops

Command: off · safety max 120 s
Relay needs real 5V on D+/VCC — not 3.3V.
At 3.3V the LEDs can light but the coil often will not click.

Recommended power (best): 12V adapter → small 5V buck → relay D+.
Share GND: buck GND + relay D− + ESP GND + 12V−.
IN → GPIO16, jumper L. Do not power the coil from ESP 3.3V.
Pump− → 12V− rail (not ESP GND — high current).
Buck needs both IN+←12V+ and IN−←12V−.
Load must be on NO (not NC). On Stop the relay red LED should go off.

Quick check: Unplug IN, touch IN→GND. You need a click, not only a red LED.
